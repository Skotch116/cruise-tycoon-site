Privacy Policy

Cruise Ship Tycoon · last updated October 2026

In short: Cruise Ship Tycoon collects no personal data. Everything you do in the game stays on your iPad.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and it does not read any of them. It has no accounts and no sign-in.

What stays on your iPad

Your saved game, your settings and your medals are saved in the game's own storage on your iPad so that you can carry on where you left off. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the game, they are deleted with it.

No internet, no ads, no tracking

The game never connects to the internet. It has no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data. It sells nothing and has no in-app purchases. We do not share information about you with anyone, because we do not have any.

Children

The game is made for the whole family, and children can play it. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included. It has no chat, no way to meet other people, no links out of the game and no ads.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes so that it collects or sends any data, this page will say so first, and the App Store privacy information will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to jibjabsmash@gmail.com.
